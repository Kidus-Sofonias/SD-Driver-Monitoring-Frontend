## The Problem — Road Safety Crisis

**Global scale:**
- 1.16 million people die on roads every year — one death every 27 seconds
- Road traffic injuries are the #1 cause of death for people aged 5-29
- 90% of deaths occur in low- and middle-income countries
- Crashes cost LMICs ~3% of GDP annually

**Ethiopia specifically:**
- Fatality rate of 37 per 100,000 population — more than double the global average (18.2)
- ~4,922 fatalities per 100,000 motor vehicles
- Young adults (15-29) and males are at highest risk
- Pedestrians account for ~30% of fatalities, motorcyclists ~16%
- Hundreds of millions of dollars drained from the economy each year

**Common causes:**
- Speeding/reckless driving (~38% of fatalities)
- Distracted driving, especially phone use (22%)
- Drunk/impaired driving (18%)
- Poor road infrastructure (14%)
- Vehicle defects (8%)

**Phone distraction is especially dangerous:**
- Dialling = 2.7× crash risk
- Reading text = 4.8× crash risk
- Sending text = 6.1× crash risk
- Reaching for phone = 12.2× crash risk

---

## The Technology Gap

Existing solutions all have barriers:
- Traditional telematics: $100-500 per vehicle + monthly fees + professional installation
- Insurance black boxes: Tied to insurers, privacy concerns, no driver feedback
- None are designed for low-income markets

Yet smartphone penetration in Africa will reach 88% by 2030. Every phone already has GPS, accelerometer, and gyroscope — the hardware is already in people's pockets.

Research shows smartphone-based driver monitoring with feedback significantly reduces risky driving (randomized controlled trials show measurable reductions in phone-induced distraction).

---

## Our Solution (Brief)

Drive Pulse turns a standard smartphone into a professional driving safety tool:
- **Zero extra hardware** — just a phone
- **AI + rules scoring** — works even without a trained model
- **3 languages** — English, Amharic, Oromo
- **For drivers and fleet operators**

## Pilot Results (from real deployments)

- 6 active driver accounts

- Full ML lifecycle operational (synthetic data → training → evaluation → promotion → auto-retrain)
- Deployed on Render with Supabase PostgreSQL
